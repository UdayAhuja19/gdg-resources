FIG. 2 THE IDEA

One sheet, one blow

It's an icebreaker, so the whole identity is ice breaking. Three moves make every layout.

ONE BLUE

Cobalt as a material

The ground is flat, heavy cobalt, like riso ink or a cyanotype. White is the light inside the ice, never a second colour.

STRUCK ONCE

One point of impact

Cracks run out from one point in straight rays and broken rings, and the pieces drift apart by a few pixels. The point sits on the comma: the pause before you say hi.

FIELD NOTES

Labelled like a study

Small spaced mono labels name what you're looking at: EVENT 001, FIG. 1, a temperature scale from −18° to +22°. The room warms up as the event goes on.

FIG. 3 COLOUR

One blue, seven temperatures

About 85% cobalt, 13% frost, 2% everything else. No second hue: Google red, yellow and green stay in the main club brand.

FIG. 4 TYPE

Giant ice, tiny notes

Archivo for everything people read, DM Mono for labels and code. The jump in size between the two is the point; don't fill the middle sizes with extra words.

FIG. 5 THE FRACTURE

Generated, never drawn

The cracks come from BlueIce.Wordmark() in blue-ice.js, so every size and every format breaks the same way. Strike it again to see another seed.

SEED 20261006
  • One impactOn a glyph that means something: the comma, the !, the 0 of 001. Never in empty space.
  • 24 rays, broken ringsRings at about 16, 38, 70, 115, 178, 262, 380, 545 and 780px from the impact on a 300px wordmark, each ±18%. They break up as they travel and are gone by 1100px.
  • Crushed centreThe three innermost rings split once more into triangles.
  • DriftShards move outward about 6.5px at the impact, under 1px at the edges, with a tiny turn. The letters move with them.
  • Linescrack, 1.6px at the impact thinning to 0.7px and fading to about 12%.
  • One seed per campaignEvery poster in a series keeps the same cracks. A new event gets a new seed.

FIG. 8 WORDS AND SCREENS

Short, warm, a little dry

We talk to students as you and about the club as we. The jokes come from the ice, never at anyone's expense.

CASING

Capitals, with two exceptions

The event name is always lowercase with its punctuation, exactly hello, world!, because it's a line of code. Running text on a website is in sentence case.

LINES WE USE

WEBSITE

Ice for the show, frost for reading

The hero, facts and footer sit on Ice; long text and forms sit on Frost. The wordmark arrives whole and cracks once, about 900ms; with reduced motion it's cracked from the start.

FIG. 10 DON'T

Keep it cold

  • No second hueFun comes from the crack and the jokes, not from adding red or yellow.
  • One impactNever two, and never in empty space.
  • No rounded cornersIce breaks with corners. Only the impact ring is round.
  • No shadowsThe only depth is light through ice: the frost glow.
  • No capitals in the nameAlways hello, world! with its comma and !.
  • No emojiAnywhere: posters, posts, captions, the site.
