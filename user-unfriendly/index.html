The brand sheet

Best UI.
Worst UX.

USER UNFRIENDLY is about the best-looking interface with the worst experience: everything looks finished, and every feature fights back. This sheet is how we show that on posts, reels, slides, certificates and the event app. It's built from the approved posters, which are further down with the files.

Posters Wordmarks and files USER-UNFRIENDLY.md

01 · The idea

Looks perfect.
Works terribly.

Design it like it ships tomorrow: clean type, a sharp layout, confident colour. Then ruin the features, the buttons, forms, sliders, captchas and pop-ups people actually have to use. The posters do it with these four moves; use one or two at a time.

Features that fight back

Buttons that lie, forms that never submit, pop-ups that won't close. CANCEL (MEANS OK) is the whole event in three words. Drawn as flat paper windows with ink title bars and hard shadows.

Can't read it, can't use it

Blur, pixelate or censor exactly what someone needs: a label, a button, the name of the event. The greys you get from blurring ink are the only greys allowed.

The facts always work

The red slab holds GOOD UI. BAD UX., the date, the time and the room. It's the one feature we never ruin.

Ruin the features.
Never the facts.

Anything on a poster, in a reel or in the app can be broken on purpose, except the facts: the date, time, room and how to sign up always work and are easy to read. If someone can't tell when and where it is, the joke is on us.

  • On screens, small text goes on paper or ink. Anything on red, or red text, is big: 24px and up, or 19px bold. The one exception is the slab's single line of DM Mono, kept from the posters.
  • No gradients, no tints, no transparency and no fourth colour. Greys only appear where ink is blurred or pixelated.
  • Shadows are hard, ink and offset down and right. On an ink background they turn red.

05 · Voice

Deadpan.
Slightly broken.

The interface is the villain, never the person. Write like a system that has stopped trying to help: short, polite and wrong in exactly the right place.

Lines we've used

  • Cancel (means OK)
  • × (decorative)
  • Title hidden for your convenience
  • Loading a website nobody can use · stuck since Tuesday
  • If you can read the title, we designed it wrong.
  • Are you sure you're sure?

The facts, plainly

  • Good UI. Bad UX.
  • Build a site that looks great and is horrible to use.
  • Tue 27 Oct · 16:00–18:00 · Room 0411
  • Come as a team or find one here. AI allowed. No experience needed. Free.
  • GDG on Campus UOBD × Computing Club

Do

  • Make every joke a broken feature: a button, a captcha, a cookie banner, a loading bar.
  • Say "team": it's a team event.
  • Put both clubs on everything: GDG on Campus UOBD × Computing Club.
  • Write USER UNFRIENDLY in capitals, or all lowercase in a blurred or tracked title.

Don't

  • Blur, track or break the date, time, room or sign-up link.
  • Make fun of real people, or name a real product as the bad one.
  • Write "solo": that was the old plan.
  • Add a fourth colour, a gradient or a soft shadow.
